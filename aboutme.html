My name is Fletcher Hunt

University

I am currently undertaking my third year of study at the Australian Catholic University (ACU) with the ambition of becoming a Design and Technology teacher. I have a strong passion for education, with particular interest in the teaching of woodworking and cabinet making skills. Alongside my academic studies, I actively engage in furniture design and construction, which has provided me with a diverse and practical skill set. These experiences inform my pedagogy and will enable me to deliver meaningful, industry-relevant learning experiences for students. My professional aspiration is to teach Industrial Technology Timber at the senior level, with a focus on preparing students for the Higher School Certificate (HSC).

Employment

I am currently employed at St Mary’s Cathedral College, Sydney, as a Learning Support Officer (LSO) and TAS Assistant. In this role, I oversee the preparation and operation of Technology and Applied Studies classrooms, including the maintenance and organisation of workshop facilities. My responsibilities also extend to budgeting, stock ordering, and resource management for both ongoing and new projects. A key aspect of my role involves working closely with Year 12 Industrial Technology Timber students, providing technical guidance, project management support, and mentorship to assist them in the successful completion of their major works.

Personal

Beyond my professional and academic commitments, I have a strong appreciation for the outdoors. I regularly engage in camping and exploring natural environments, particularly in the Blue Mountains and coastal regions, which provide me with balance, perspective, and inspiration.
