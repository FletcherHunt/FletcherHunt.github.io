Overview/General Overview

My teaching style is grounded in a strong belief that students learn most effectively through experiential, hands-on engagement. I am passionate about creating opportunities for students to actively participate in their learning, with a focus on developing both practical skills and problem-solving abilities. I place great importance on demonstrations, as they allow students to observe processes directly, ask questions in real time, and gain clarity before applying techniques independently. I also encourage students to take ownership of their learning by engaging in practical tasks that challenge their abilities. Where tasks may appear straightforward, I aim to extend students through enrichment opportunities and differentiated challenges, ensuring that every learner remains engaged, motivated, and supported in their growth.

Technology Mandatory (Years 7–8)

In Technology Mandatory, I see my role as providing students with broad exposure to different areas of design and technology. My teaching style emphasises demonstrations and guided practice, which help students grasp new skills quickly across areas such as food, timber, textiles, and digital technologies. I encourage students to explore and experiment, while also ensuring they understand the importance of safety, problem-solving, and design thinking. By challenging students to extend their projects beyond the basics, I aim to foster curiosity and give them the confidence to engage with more specialised Technology subjects in later years.

Industrial Technology Timber (Years 7–10)

In the junior years, I introduce students to the fundamentals of working with timber through clear demonstrations and guided practice. I believe it is important for students to see processes modelled in person so they can ask questions and build confidence before applying techniques independently. My teaching style places a strong emphasis on learning by doing, which allows students to engage directly with tools, materials, and construction methods.
